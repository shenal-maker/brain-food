# Brain Food

A Chrome extension that blocks distracting sites until you study. Built for people who want to build technical intuition without becoming engineers — founders, investors, policy folks, and the technically curious.

## How it works

1. **Block distracting sites** — Add sites to your block list. They stay blocked until you complete a study session.
2. **Watch a 10-minute video segment** — Each course breaks a long lecture into digestible 10-minute chunks with specific topics.
3. **Take a Kahoot-style quiz** — Timed questions with streaks, scoring, and confetti. Score 8/10+ to master a set.
4. **Repeat daily** — A 30-day consistency heatmap tracks your streak. Showing up matters more than getting every answer right.

## Features

- **Course-based learning** — Structured courses with video segments and quiz sets
- **Kahoot-style quizzes** — Timed questions, streak multipliers, sound effects, confetti
- **Consistency heatmap** — 30-day grid showing your study days
- **Smart blocking** — Configurable browse timer with gentle nudge before full block
- **Notes per video** — Jot down key concepts while watching
- **"I stopped at" timestamps** — Pick up where you left off
- **Project prompts** — After each quiz, get a "Show Your Work" prompt to apply what you learned
- **Share on Twitter** — One-click sharing with a pre-filled tweet
- **XP and streaks** — Lightweight gamification to keep momentum

## Install

1. Clone this repo
2. Go to `chrome://extensions`
3. Enable **Developer mode**
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder
5. Add distracting sites in the extension popup
6. Start learning

## Philosophy

Inspired by Austin Kleon's *Show Your Work* — learning is better when you share it. The extension nudges you toward building in public: take what you learned, turn it into a tweet or a short post, and put it out there. You don't have to be an expert. You just have to show up.
